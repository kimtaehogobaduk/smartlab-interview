## 2026-03-30 - Optimized Leaderboard Calculation Complexity
**Learning:** In interview scoring systems like SmartLab, `buildLeaderboard` is re-evaluated frequently as evaluation submissions arrive. Naively filtering `submissions` per candidate ($O(N \cdot S)$) and performing nested `.find()` array operations inside criteria loops ($O(K^2 \cdot M)$) creates a noticeable bottleneck as candidates and evaluation criteria grow. Pre-grouping submissions into a `Map` ($O(S)$), using single-pass accumulators, pre-computing primary tie-breaker scores, and direct item tracking reduced algorithmic complexity from $O(N \cdot S + N \cdot K^2 \cdot M)$ down to $O(S + N \cdot K + N \log N)$.
**Action:** Always pre-group relational data with `Map` before candidate/room iteration, and pre-compute comparison metrics before sorting.
